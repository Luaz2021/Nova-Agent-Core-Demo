# Acme SaaS brand.md

This is a fictional example. It demonstrates the `brand.md` format and should not be treated as official guidance for a real brand.

## Brand Identity

### Positioning

- One-line position: calm workflow software for teams that manage complex approvals.
- We are: precise, dependable, and quietly helpful.
- We are not: flashy automation theater or generic productivity hype.

### Purpose And Values

- Purpose: help teams move important work forward without losing the reason behind a decision.
- Clarity: make status, ownership, and next action visible.
- Trust: preserve review history and decision context.
- Restraint: remove noise instead of adding more dashboards.

## Voice System

### Voice Identity

- Brand analogy: an operations lead who keeps the room moving without raising their voice.
- Traits: calm, exact, supportive, plainspoken.
- Not this: peppy, overconfident, or filled with productivity cliches.

### Behavioral Rules

- Use short sentences in product UI.
- Prefer concrete nouns over abstract promises.
- Say what changed, who owns it, and what happens next.
- Avoid "unlock," "supercharge," and "10x."

### Tone Modulation

- Product UI: direct, low emotion, under 12 words.
- Onboarding: warm, practical, under 18 words.
- Long-form: explanatory and source-backed.

## Visual Foundation

### color.primary.green

- Hex: #184F35
- AI-optimized name: dark British racing green
- Perceptual descriptors: deep, cool, muted, natural
- Mood: grounded, reliable, calm
- Usage: primary CTAs, headings, critical status emphasis
- Avoid confusing with: olive, bright forest green, neon green

### type.interface

- Family: Inter
- Classification: humanist sans-serif
- Character: legible, practical, neutral, open
- Comparable fonts: SF Pro, IBM Plex Sans, Helvetica Neue
- Usage: product UI, tables, forms, and workflow labels

### Logo Rules

- Use only approved logo artwork.
- Do not generate or approximate the logo in AI image tools.
- Keep clearspace equal to the height of the mark.

## Media DNA

### Imagery

- Style: real workspaces, quiet focus, natural daylight.
- Avoid: staged smiles, overlit offices, fake holographic UI, exaggerated celebration.
- Prompt fragment: candid team review session, soft directional daylight, natural materials, practical workspace, documentary style.

## AI Execution Rules

- Use the voice rules before drafting product copy.
- Use color names and prompt fragments for image generation, not hex values alone.
- Ask for clarification before inventing industry-specific claims.
