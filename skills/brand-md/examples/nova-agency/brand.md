# Nova Agency brand.md

This is a fictional example. It demonstrates the `brand.md` format and should not be treated as official guidance for a real brand.

## Brand Identity

### Positioning

- One-line position: a strategic design studio for ambitious climate and infrastructure companies.
- We are: editorial, rigorous, and unusually practical.
- We are not: trend-led, mystical, or style-first.

### Purpose And Values

- Purpose: help complex organizations communicate with force and credibility.
- Evidence: strategy starts from source material.
- Taste: visual decisions should feel intentional, not fashionable.
- Momentum: make the next decision easier.

## Voice System

### Voice Identity

- Brand analogy: a sharp editorial director with a systems mind.
- Traits: incisive, literate, concrete, composed.
- Not this: agency-speak, vague provocation, or inflated manifesto language.

### Behavioral Rules

- Lead with the point.
- Use metaphor sparingly and only when it clarifies.
- Prefer exact claims over broad ambition.
- Avoid "bold," "disruptive," "human-centered," and "future-proof" unless source context demands them.

## Visual Foundation

### color.ink.black

- Hex: #111111
- AI-optimized name: near-black editorial ink
- Perceptual descriptors: crisp, grounded, high-contrast
- Mood: decisive, serious, premium
- Usage: primary text, editorial headlines, monochrome identity moments

### color.signal.blue

- Hex: #2F6BFF
- AI-optimized name: vivid cobalt blue
- Perceptual descriptors: electric, clean, cool, technical
- Mood: intelligent, modern, alert
- Usage: active states, links, diagrams, strategic emphasis
- Avoid confusing with: navy, sky blue, purple-blue gradient

### type.editorial

- Family: IBM Plex Serif
- Classification: transitional serif
- Character: serious, editorial, technical, readable
- Comparable fonts: Source Serif, Tiempos Text, Georgia
- Usage: long-form thinking, reports, case studies

## Media DNA

### Imagery

- Style: infrastructure, material detail, documents, field research, technical environments.
- Avoid: abstract gradient art, generic laptop shots, climate stock imagery.
- Prompt fragment: editorial documentary image, infrastructure detail, field research materials, natural overcast light, restrained composition.

## AI Execution Rules

- Do not over-warm the voice.
- Keep claims tied to evidence or project context.
- Use visual language that feels editorial and field-based, not decorative.
