# Fold brand.md

This is a fictional example. It demonstrates the `brand.md` format and should not be treated as official guidance for a real brand.

## Brand Identity

### Positioning

- One-line position: everyday home storage that makes small spaces feel calm.
- We are: warm, clever, tactile, and quietly optimistic.
- We are not: luxury minimalism, plastic utility, or chaotic lifestyle content.

### Purpose And Values

- Purpose: make order feel accessible and pleasant.
- Ease: organization should not require a personality change.
- Tactility: materials and details should feel good to use.
- Calm: visual and verbal choices should reduce pressure.

## Voice System

### Voice Identity

- Brand analogy: a practical friend who knows where everything goes.
- Traits: warm, simple, reassuring, lightly witty.
- Not this: moralizing, perfectionist, or overly cute.

### Behavioral Rules

- Use everyday language.
- Make organization feel achievable.
- Avoid shame-based phrases like "clutter problem."
- Keep wit small and functional.

## Visual Foundation

### color.canvas.oat

- Hex: #E8DDC8
- AI-optimized name: warm oat canvas
- Perceptual descriptors: soft, warm, natural, matte
- Mood: calm, tactile, domestic
- Usage: backgrounds, packaging fields, lifestyle surfaces
- Avoid confusing with: beige luxury minimalism, yellow cream, cold grey

### color.accent.tomato

- Hex: #D54A33
- AI-optimized name: muted tomato red
- Perceptual descriptors: warm, grounded, friendly, slightly earthy
- Mood: cheerful, human, practical
- Usage: small accents, labels, moments of orientation

### type.packaging

- Family: DM Sans
- Classification: geometric humanist sans-serif
- Character: rounded, clear, friendly, modern
- Comparable fonts: Avenir, Nunito Sans, Circular
- Usage: packaging, product labels, short interface copy

## Media DNA

### Imagery

- Style: small homes, soft natural light, tactile materials, hands using products.
- Avoid: impossible pristine rooms, luxury marble kitchens, heavy shadows, artificial smiles.
- Prompt fragment: small apartment storage detail, warm natural window light, tactile fabric bins, lived-in calm, editorial product photography.

## AI Execution Rules

- Keep copy reassuring rather than corrective.
- Use material and tactile descriptors for images.
- Do not make the brand feel premium in a cold or status-driven way.
